Use the hammock, living room and quieter corners of the home to rest, journal, listen to music, read or simply settle.

Books

ORIENTATION + SAFETY

A new place can take a moment to feel familiar.

La Luz del Bosque is a very safe environment. At the same time, arriving somewhere new can temporarily stir the nervous system simply because the sounds, rhythms, people and landscape are unfamiliar.

If something feels unusual or you are unsure what you are hearing or seeing, please reach out rather than letting your imagination fill in the blanks. We can be available by phone, message or walkie-talkie when one is provided.

If there is an actual emergency, call us immediately or come to the main house. For ordinary questions or moments of uncertainty, please message or call rather than dropping by the house unexpectedly. This helps us stay available while also maintaining the privacy and rhythm of our own home.

WILDLIFE + SMALL CREATURES

Nature is not looking for a fight.

This is a living tropical landscape. Most wildlife wants space, not interaction. Awareness is usually enough: look where you step, avoid reaching blindly into hidden spaces, keep food and debris tidy, and let wild things remain wild.

Snakes

Wear closed shoes in taller grass, on trails and after dark. Use a flashlight or headlamp in low light. If you see a snake, stop, give it plenty of room and back away slowly. Do not touch, corner, catch or kill it. If one is near the home, contact us.

Scorpions

Scorpions like dark, protected places. Shake out shoes, clothing or towels left on the floor or outside. Avoid putting bare hands into boxes, stored materials, wood piles or debris.

Ants

Some ants bite or sting, including bullet ants and other stinging species. Do not stand on, sit beside or disturb an active nest or trail. If a moving column passes through, simply give it space and let it move on.

If bitten by a snake: seek emergency medical care immediately. Do not cut or suck the bite, apply ice, use a tourniquet or try to capture the snake. In Costa Rica, call 9-1-1.
SOUNDS OF THE LAND

Some sounds may be new to you.

Neighbors + Road

The road beside the Container Home is a private shared road. Our neighbors at Diamante Luz are a small community of friends nearby, so you may occasionally hear voices or a car passing.

Wild Sounds

Coyotes live farther down the road and are heard far more often than seen. Howler monkeys can sound incredibly loud and dramatic. There is also a large chorus of birds whose calls may be unfamiliar.

Daily Rhythm

Sunset is relatively consistent throughout the year, and many people naturally begin waking earlier and feeling sleepy around 8:30–9:00 pm.

We have not had problems with the animals or neighborhood sounds described here. If something feels unfamiliar and your system starts telling a frightening story, feel free to check in with us so we can help you orient to what is actually happening.

COOLING + POWER

A few practical rhythms of the home.

Cooling the Space

Heat rises, so the upstairs bedroom can be warmer during the middle of the day. Open the windows and use the fan for airflow. The bedroom may feel best in the early morning and evening, with the lower living spaces more comfortable during warmer hours.

Power Outages

Short power outages are common in this area, especially in rainy weather. A flashlight will be accessible in the home. We also recommend keeping your own headlamp or flashlight where you can find it easily after dark.

DOGS ON THE LAND

You may have a few friendly visitors.

Our dogs live on the property and may wander by to say hello. They are not allowed inside the Container Home. If they try to come in, please lovingly guide them back outside and keep the doorway clear. Think of yourself as a temporary auntie or uncle helping them remember the house rules.

RECYCLING + FOOD WASTE

A different rhythm for waste.

Recycling

Hard and soft plastics, cans, glass and accepted recyclables go into the recycling bin. Please rinse or wash and fully dry items before placing them inside.

Food + Compost

Food scraps go into the separate food-waste container. Appropriate scraps are taken to the chickens.

Bathroom

The bathroom bin is for used toilet paper only. No wipes, sanitary products, wrappers or other plastics. Feminine-product wrappers go into recycling.

LAUNDRY

Laundry is simple.

For now, please communicate with us if you need laundry done and we can arrange it with you. A washer and dryer may eventually be available directly at the Container Home; if so, updated instructions will be provided here.

HOUSE AGREEMENTS

Simple agreements help the home + land stay well.

• Keep food sealed and surfaces reasonably clean so insects and wildlife are not invited indoors.

• Do not feed or handle wildlife.

• Please keep our dogs outside the Container Home.

• No parties, events or unregistered overnight guests without prior agreement.

• Respect quiet around the shared land, neighboring properties and the plantel.

• Ask before harvesting unfamiliar plants.

• Let us know promptly about leaks, breakage, electrical issues or anything that needs attention.

IF YOU ARE HERE FOR AN IMMERSION

Let the home be part of the practice.

The Container Home is not simply where you sleep between sessions. It is also a place for rest, nourishment, journaling, quiet, movement, integration and listening. You do not need to fill every open hour. Spaciousness is intentionally part of the immersion.

Let us know if your body needs more rest, food, quiet or pacing than originally imagined. The arc is responsive rather than rigid.

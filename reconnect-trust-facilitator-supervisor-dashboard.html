SESSION CHOICE COMPASS

Where do I go today?

BODY / SOMA

choose when

She is tired, scattered, highly verbal, disconnected from sensation, physically braced, or needs grounding more than meaning.

Ask: What does the body need before we ask it to tell us more?

BOTANICAL / AROMA

choose when

Direct conversation feels like too much; scent creates curiosity, settling or access without requiring a story; she has enough capacity for sensory engagement.

Ask: What changes when the plant enters the conversation?

RELATIONAL

choose when

A live pattern is happening between you, or themes of attachment, approval, boundaries, pursuit / withdrawal, yes / no or self-abandonment are present.

Ask: What is happening between us right now?

MOVEMENT / CORE

choose when

There is mobilizable energy, anger, inhibition, collapse, a repeated posture / gesture, or a belief that can be explored through movement without pushing intensity.

Ask: If the body could complete one small movement, what would it want to do?

DEEPER / INTUITIVE

choose when

She is oriented, nourished, present, curious and able to move toward and away from material without becoming overwhelmed.

Ask: Is this revealing itself—or am I trying to mine it up?

REST / FOOD / LAND

choose when

Low intake, exhaustion, confusion, flatness, escalating activation, inability to track sensation, or “I don't know” feels more like depletion than spaciousness.

Ask: Would less be more responsible right now?

DAILY SUPERVISOR CARD

Five questions before every session

1. Has she eaten, hydrated and rested enough for this?
2. What is her body showing me before her story begins?
3. What doorway requires the least force?
4. Can she choose, pause, orient and come back into contact?
5. What would make this session complete enough—not impressive?

After

BODY • IMAGE • WORDS • SESSION • THREAD

What settled? What activated? What needs no more attention today? What thread—if any—travels forward?

FACILITATOR BOUNDARY

Body Oracle is not a substitute for medical or psychiatric care. Use intuitive impressions as invitations for inquiry, not diagnoses. Do not change medications or present Aroma Point Therapy, bodywork, movement or intuitive work as treatment for a medical or psychiatric condition. If eating / hydration, orientation, functioning or safety significantly deteriorates, pause the immersion process and involve appropriate clinical support.

01 · The central pulse
How do we make room for who we are becoming?
From protection through participation into possibility.

For those navigating the exhaustion of healing, the erosion of self-trust, and the longing to feel more fully alive within the life they're living.

Edit the central inquiry freely. The rest of this map can evolve with it.

02 · The living axis

Protection · Relationship · Participation · Possibility

A recurring movement, not a rigid progression. Protection is not the enemy.

Protection

How life has taught us to survive, anticipate, adapt, and guard ourselves.

Relationship

The meeting place: reciprocity, discernment, sensation, and conscious witnessing.

Participation

Engaging with life through presence, choice, contact, and response.

Possibility

Room for becoming, even when pain or uncertainty remains.

03 · The four movements

The essential inquiries

These four parts shape the masterclass and recur throughout all seven months.

I · REMEMBERING

Original Nature

What remains alive within me, even now?

Innocence, essence, imagination, vitality, the Heart–Kidney dialogue, and the possibility of remembering without returning to the past.

What qualities have remained with me through everything I've lived?

II · RECOGNIZING

Protection & the Search for Repair

How have my experiences shaped the ways I protect myself?

Shock, grief, illness, chronic healing, self-doubt, hypervigilance, information overload, and the ways listening can become monitoring.

When does caring for myself become another demand placed upon me?

III · RELATING

Reciprocity & Discernment

How can I meet what is here without immediately needing to change it?

Psychic-somatic anchoring, contact with sensation, intuition grounded in the soma, supportive practices, and knowing when to seek practical or clinical care.

What needs attention? What needs permission to unfold?

IV · REORGANIZING

Participation & Possibility

What becomes possible when I respond from presence and choice?

Imagination, original nature as living relationship, practical participation, flexible protection, and the unfolding of becoming.

What is available to me here and now that nourishes life?

05 · The inner ecology

From extraction to reciprocity

A secondary lens—not necessarily the main title of the journey.

When healing becomes extraction

What is wrong with me?
What does every sensation mean?
Which protocol will finally fix me?
How do I get back to who I was?
What more must I do?

When healing becomes relationship

What needs care or attention?
What can I notice without decoding?
What support and evidence guide my choices?
Who am I becoming through what I've lived?
What is already available to me?

This work does not replace evaluation or treatment of physical symptoms. Reciprocity can include medical care, practical help, community, rest, food, plants, movement, and the resources already at hand.

06 · The seven-month immersion

Landscapes of exploration

Expand each month to edit the details. The four movements above repeat within every month.

1 · Crown & Anchor

Original nature, shock, protection, Heart–Kidney dialogue, and psychic-somatic anchoring.
Exploratory vessel pairing: Ren Mai + Yin Qiao (LU-7 / KI-6).
Inquiry: What remains alive in me beneath and alongside my protective adaptations?

2 · Water

The right to exist; safety, sensitivity, fear, will, wisdom.
Exploratory pairing: Du Mai + Yang Qiao (SI-3 / BL-62).
Inquiry: What helps me experience ground and choice when fear is present?

3 · Earth

The right to need and receive; nourishment, belonging, and support.
Exploratory pairing: Chong Mai + Yin Wei (SP-4 / PC-6).
Inquiry: What would it mean to receive without needing to earn it?

4 · Wood

The right to choose and act; agency, boundaries, anger, expression.
Exploratory pairing: Dai Mai + Yang Wei (GB-41 / SJ-5).
Inquiry: How can my life force move without overriding my limits?

5 · Fire

The right to love, express, and be seen; joy, intimacy, Shen, relational contact.
Revisit vessel practices as appropriate.
Inquiry: Can I be visible while remaining in contact with myself?

6 · Metal

The right to grieve and release; dignity, boundaries, Po, letting go.
Revisit vessel practices as appropriate.
Inquiry: What is ready to be honored, and what might be released?

7 · Integration

Mask, lower self, higher self, core; choice, discernment, reciprocity, and living practice.
Inquiry: What is different in how I participate in life now?

Note: Vessel pairings are traditional point-pair associations used here as exploratory teaching design, not established correspondences to trauma or character structures. Five Elements and Core Energetics structures are distinct lenses, not one-to-one mappings.

08 · The origin and the harvest

Why this work exists

The initiation: my mother's illness and death, grief, spiritual opening, and the emergence of subtle perception.

The long passage: sixteen years of seeking, supporting others, navigating symptoms, learning, and uncertainty.

The present threshold: a quieter life, fewer external resources, and the inquiry: What is already here that can support relationship, vitality, and becoming?

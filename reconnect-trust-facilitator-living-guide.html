Pocket Compass

Before every encounter

Ask: What is actually here? What does her body appear to have capacity for? Does this moment want contact, nourishment, movement, rest, witnessing, or space?

When unsure: Orient → Notice → Ask → Offer choice → Listen again.

Remember: Do not confuse intensity with depth. The goal is not to produce an experience. The practice is relationship with what is revealing itself.

MondayARRIVELand before story.

Intention

Orient + establish relationship.

Facilitator Remember

Nothing to accomplish.

Suggested Flow

Casita → settle → tea / nourishment → welcome apothecary → land orientation → opening ritual → dinner → rest.

Opening + Ritual

“There is nothing you need to accomplish today. We are beginning by noticing that you are here—on this land, in this body, at this moment in your life.”

Lineage naming: I am _____. I am the daughter of _____ + _____. I am the granddaughter of _____, _____, _____ + _____. I arrive carrying what has brought me this far. I do not need to know yet what I am becoming.

Plant ally: invite attention rather than selection. Witnessing Eye: create an image/object representing the part of self capable of lovingly staying with the part that wants to escape, loop, grasp or disappear.

Prompts

• What do you notice now that you're actually here?

• Where does your body already feel even slightly more settled?

• What needs nothing from you tonight?

• As we walk, what plant, place, sound, or living being keeps drawing your attention?

Watch For

Travel fatigue • performing wellness • urgency to start the work • low nourishment • anxiety disguised as enthusiasm. Keep arrival simple.

Closing Thread

Return to the Witnessing Eye. What has changed in the relationship between the witnessing self and the part that reaches for escape, repetition or old attachment? What promise is realistic enough to keep?

Prompts

• What became more true this week?

• What do you want to practice rather than merely understand?

• What support will this need when ordinary life returns?

• What can remain unfinished?

Watch For

Grand promises • urgency to reinvent life • adding more work because ending feels vulnerable • mistaking retreat-state relief for permanent change.

SundayRETURNIntegration in motion.

Intention

Carry practice into life.

Facilitator Remember

Transition is part of the immersion.

Suggested Flow

Early departure → optional river / stone ritual → contact dance / Dance Church → onward transition.

Stone / River Ritual

Let the stone hold a strategy, agreement, burden or story that once served but no longer needs to organize life. Acknowledge what it protected; place the stone down without rejecting the part of self that once needed it.

Prompts

• What are you willing to stop carrying in the same way?

• What are you not leaving behind?

• How can movement become listening rather than performance?

• What will returning to yourself look like on an ordinary Tuesday?

Watch For

Emotional drop after closing • overstimulation • fatigue • pressure for catharsis • difficulty moving from being held toward autonomy.

Facilitator Safety Compass

The immersion does not need to hold what requires clinical care.

Significant deterioration in eating or hydration, inability to care for self, severe confusion or disorganization, escalating substance use, suicidality / self-harm risk, or other acute symptoms are reasons to pause immersion work and involve appropriate clinical support.

Medication boundary: medication changes belong with the prescribing clinician.

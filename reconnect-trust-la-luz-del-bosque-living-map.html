The House

The immersion is a living map, not a rigid retreat schedule.

Each day has a rhythm and a doorway. The work is curated around what is actually present, with enough structure to feel held and enough space for the body, land and relationship to lead.

Recommended Arc

Seven movements through the house

1. ARRIVE

Settle into the space. Orient to the land. Share a meal. Begin with a Body Oracle arrival + intuitive listening session that asks: What is here? What wants support? What does trust mean right now?

2. REGULATE

Hands-on structural bodywork, slow movement and nervous-system settling. The emphasis is not “fixing” but giving the body enough support to become perceptible again.

3. LISTEN

Nature time, sensory practice, rest and spacious integration. A day with less intervention can be part of the work.

4. RELATE

Restoration of Trust + Relational Sanctuary practices: noticing activation, choice, contact, boundaries, staying, leaving, returning and speaking from present experience.

5. REMEMBER

Aroma Point / botanical body session. Meet selected oils and plants slowly. If ancestry naturally emerges and there is enough capacity, Ancestral Witnessing may become one possible doorway.

6. INTEGRATE

Choose the session that best meets what has surfaced: Body Oracle, relational work, bodywork, botanical work, movement, nature or spaciousness.

7. RETURN

Closing reflection + Integration Map. Name what is coming home, what practices continue, what plant or oil becomes a companion, and what support is needed next. Dance Church may serve as a threshold back into community and movement.

Nourishment

Food that steadies rather than performs wellness

The food rhythm is simple and adaptable. Juice or fruit can be part of breakfast, but the default is regular meals with protein, fat, fiber and carbohydrates rather than a liquid-heavy “detox” structure.

MorningEggs + avocado + tortilla/cassava; oatmeal with nuts and fruit; yogurt/chia bowl with nut butter; tea; optional juice alongside food.
MiddaySelf-serve leftovers, soup, rice and beans, roasted vegetables, hummus, eggs, chicken/fish if desired, fruit and nuts.
EveningOne nourishing communal meal: curry + rice, soup/stew, roasted protein + roots + greens, tacos/bowls, rice/beans/vegetables.
PrincipleKeep the kitchen easy enough that hosting does not become the main job.
Guest Experience

What to expect

This is not a program where every hour is filled. It is a private, relationally held immersion with a recommended arc and a menu of doorways. Together we choose what is most supportive as the week unfolds. The intention is not to force transformation, but to create enough contact, nourishment, rhythm and spaciousness for something true to become audible.

This immersion is not a substitute for medical, psychiatric, addiction or eating-disorder treatment. Participation may require appropriate outside clinical support depending on current health, medication/substance use, nutritional status and psychological stability.
